Our story

Built from a love of what clothing can become.

The beginning

A personal interest, stitched into every next step.

D’REGAL began with early memories of watching a mother sew and a growing fascination with how a garment comes together. That exposure became a personal interest in fashion, clothing creation and the freedom of making something feel right for the person wearing it.

Today, D’REGAL is taking shape as a contemporary fashion house with an elevated streetwear identity. The point of view is confident, versatile and open to evolution — from relaxed ready-made pieces to future custom work.

We believe the clothes you choose can hold comfort, individuality and intention at the same time.

What guides us

Expression

Pieces designed to be interpreted, restyled and made personal.

Craft

A respect for the making process and the thoughtful details behind clothing.

Possibility

A fashion house in motion, with room to grow into new forms and categories.
