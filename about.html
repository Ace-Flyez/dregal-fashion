Where it begins

A love for making.
A space to become.

D’REGAL began with early memories of a mother who sewed, and a growing fascination with how clothing comes together. That early influence became a personal interest in fashion, garment creation and the feeling of making something that feels right for the person wearing it.

Today, D’REGAL is taking shape as a contemporary fashion brand with room to evolve. The first edit explores relaxed everyday pieces, but the brand is not tied to one category or aesthetic. It is a wider point of view that can move across different expressions of contemporary style.

We believe clothing can be comfortable without feeling ordinary, expressive without feeling forced, and considered without being out of reach.
